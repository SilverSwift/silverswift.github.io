Hey there!

It looks like patch [2.9.16.10523](https://www.digitalcombatsimulator.com/en/news/changelog/release/2.9.16.10523/) messed CMS programms for F/A-18C.

By default, MAN 2 is used on CMS AFT after the ALE-47 is initially switched from STBY to MAN 1.
After manually selecting a CMS program using the STEP button, the displayed program is used.
For CMS FWD, MAN 6 is used instead of MAN 5.
Additionally, the Panic program (sidewall button) has been moved to AUTO 1.

A workaround for the F/A-18C is in a patch 1.0.1. 

Those considerations are also helpfull to understand how to use DTC in missions.

It can be downloaded from the github: [v1.0.1](https://github.com/SilverSwift/DCS_CMS_Editor/releases/tag/v1.0.1)
